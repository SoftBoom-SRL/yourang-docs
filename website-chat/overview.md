# The ChatBot

What the ChatBot does, what you need and how it goes live.

_The ChatBot is an assistant that appears in a corner of your website and answers visitors in writing, day and night. It uses the instructions, documents and tools you give it, with your logo and your colours._

## What it does <a id="cosa-fa"></a>

- **Answers right away** — Opening hours, services, prices, how to reach you: the visitor gets an answer in seconds, without waiting for an email.
- **Uses your content** — It reads the Knowledge base documents and follows your instructions, like the platform's other assistants.
- **Hands you anyone who asks for a person** — If the visitor wants to talk to someone, the ChatBot says so, asks for a name and a contact, and the chat lands among those to handle.
- **Speaks the visitor's language** — It replies in the language the visitor writes in.
- **Reads your website** — When the instructions and the Knowledge base are not enough, it opens the pages of your allowed site on the spot and answers with what they say today, with the link to the right page.
- **Says it is an AI assistant** — The visitor always sees they are writing to a virtual assistant and can ask for a person, as the EU Artificial Intelligence Act requires.

## How it goes live <a id="come-funziona"></a>

1. **Create the ChatBot** — In Assistants, create a new ChatBot. It starts ready to use, with a basic look and your company's website already allowed.
2. **Write the instructions** — In the Instructions tab, explain how it should answer and what it must never say.
3. **Choose the look (optional)** — In the Website tab, change the title, logo, colour, welcome message and suggested questions, then publish. You can also do it later.
4. **Turn it on and paste the code** — At the top of the Website tab: turn the ChatBot on, copy the code and paste it into your site. You'll find where it goes with WordPress, Wix, Shopify, Squarespace and Google Tag Manager.
5. **Check that it works** — Open a page of your site: the check in the tab completes by itself. If the code is on a site that isn't allowed yet, you allow it in one click.

## What you need <a id="cosa-serve"></a>

- **Nothing to pay** — The ChatBot is free. Answers use tokens: every organization has 50 million a month, with an hourly limit so a traffic spike can't use up the month in an afternoon. Usage is shown in the Website tab.
- **Access to your website** — To paste one line of code into its pages, yourself or with whoever runs the site.

> [!TIP]
> **When the tokens run out**
> The ChatBot stops answering until next month (or the next hour, for the hourly limit): the visitor reads that the team will reply, and their message stays in the conversations. You get a notice at 80% of the month's tokens and when they run out. Tests in the Website tab use the month's tokens too.

## Which artificial intelligence it uses <a id="intelligenza"></a>

The ChatBot uses the platform's artificial intelligence, free within the month's tokens. Soon you'll be able to use another provider's model with your own keys, without using the month's tokens.
