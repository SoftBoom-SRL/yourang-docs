# FAQs: ready answers

Write the answers to the questions customers ask most, import a list, and delete several FAQs at once.

_A FAQ is a question with the answer you want your assistant to give. When a customer asks something similar, the assistant replies with your text. You manage them on the FAQ page of the Knowledge base._

## Add a question <a id="aggiungere-domanda"></a>

1. **Open the FAQ page** — In the Knowledge base, open the FAQ page. Only members who can manage knowledge see the buttons to add, edit and delete.
2. **Write the question and the answer** — Click “New question”. Fill in “Customer's question” (10 to 500 characters) and “Assistant's answer” (10 to 2000 characters). Write the answer the way you would say it out loud, in a few sentences.
3. **Save** — Click “Add FAQ”. To change one later, use “Edit” on its card and then “Save changes”.

## Import a list <a id="importare-elenco"></a>

If you already have a list, “Import from file” opens a window with two tabs:

- **Structured file** — CSV or JSON, up to 2 MB. Imported instantly, with no daily limit. You can download an example of each format.
- **AI from document** — PDF or TXT, up to 25 MB. The AI extracts the FAQs from the text. These imports have a daily limit: the tab shows how many are left today.

Before confirming you review every row: it is marked as New, Updated answer or Already exists, and rows with a problem (missing or too long question or answer, duplicate in the file) are flagged. You can remove a row or add a new one in the preview.

## Search and delete several at once <a id="cercare-ed-eliminare"></a>

The search box looks in questions and answers. Each FAQ card has a checkbox, and “Select all” ticks every FAQ shown by the current search; if only some are ticked, the box shows a partial mark. “Delete N selected” asks for confirmation and removes them in one go: the assistant stops using them and it cannot be undone.

> [!TIP]
> **Find what is missing**
> On the page, ask the assistant “What is missing from my FAQs?” or “What do customers ask most?”: it looks at your FAQs and your latest calls.
