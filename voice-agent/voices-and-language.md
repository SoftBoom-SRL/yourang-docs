# Models and voices: use cases

Compare model behaviour, choose a voice and fine-tune the settings.

Compare models in the compact selector under Model and voice. This guide explains use cases and limitations. After choosing a model, the catalogue shows compatible voices; search remains global. Save the combination before opening Advanced settings. Configure languages in Instructions.

The page presents models in a compact selector. Choose the model for the conversation you want to offer, then listen to its voices. These suggestions are starting points: several models may suit the same need. Try them with real customer requests.

_The agent's voice and language are crucial to creating a natural, welcoming experience. Yourang supports multiple voices and languages to fit your customers._

## Choosing a model: use cases and limitations <a id="model-use-cases"></a>

### GPT Live 1 <a id="model-zeta"></a>

Choose it if… you want conversations with spontaneous interruptions and corrections.

What you can adjust here: Listening cues, patience during pauses, tone and formality. Style and pauses guide behaviour through instructions.

Worth knowing: Listening cues and pauses are guidance, not guaranteed frequencies. Listening cues such as “mm-hm” do not confirm bookings or other actions. Interrupting speech does not automatically cancel a tool that has already started.

A scenario to try: Request a Tuesday appointment, then interrupt with “No, Thursday”. Check that it accepts the correction before confirming.

### GPT Realtime <a id="model-base"></a>

Choose it if… you want to adjust pauses, interruptions and speed.

What you can adjust here: Speaking speed, silence duration or turn endings based on sentence meaning. Tone and formality too.

Worth knowing: Continuous listening is available with GPT Live.

A scenario to try: Dictate a number with a pause halfway through. Check that it waits, then try correcting it while it answers.

### Gemini Live <a id="model-beta"></a>

Good to know: With Gemini you also choose the platform. Gemini Stable is recommended for phone calls and keeps data in Europe; Gemini 3.8 is the newer model, but on the phone it can misunderstand short sentences.

Choose it if… you want to give customers time to think and explain.

What you can adjust here: Silence duration, response variation, tone and formality. A candidate to try for detailed requests and explanations.

Worth knowing: Numeric speaking speed cannot be adjusted here.

A scenario to try: Explain a problem, pause for two seconds, then add a detail. Does it let you finish? Try a follow-up question too.

### Eleven v3 Conversational <a id="model-gamma"></a>

Choose it if… you want to find a voice that fits your business.

What you can adjust here: Tone, formality and response variation. Listen to several voices saying the same greeting to find the right character.

Worth knowing: Not all provider expressiveness features are available here.

A scenario to try: Ask two voices to deliver the same welcome and respond to an unhappy customer. Assess clarity, warmth and fit with your business.

### Nova Sonic <a id="model-delta"></a>

Choose it if… you want to welcome customers in the seven supported languages.

What you can adjust here: Style and response variation. Polyglot voices can retain their vocal identity across supported languages.

Worth knowing: Arabic and Chinese excluded; speed and pauses cannot be adjusted here.

A scenario to try: Start a request in Italian, then continue in English. Check that it retains the details and that pronunciation suits your customers.

### Grok Voice Think Fast 2.0 <a id="model-epsilon"></a>

Choose it if… you want to adjust speaking pace for numbers and instructions.

What you can adjust here: Speaking speed, speech sensitivity and silence duration, alongside common style settings.

Worth knowing: Pronunciation dictionaries are not available here.

A scenario to try: Ask for three steps and a code to be repeated. Try a slower speed and check that it remains clear and easy to interrupt.

## Choosing the voice <a id="scelta-voce"></a>

Pick a synthesised voice that reflects your brand's tone. Available voices vary in gender, accent, and character.

1. Listen to previews of each available voice
2. Choose the one that best represents your business
3. Test the voice in trial conversations
4. Adjust the settings available for the selected model

## Supported languages <a id="lingue-supportate"></a>

Yourang supports the following languages for the voice assistant:

- Italian (IT), default
- English (EN), for international customers
- French (FR), for visitors from France and Switzerland
- German (DE), for visitors from Germany and Austria
- Spanish (ES), for visitors from Spain and Latin America

> [!NOTE]
> **Multiple languages**
> Configure multiple languages supported by the model. The assistant recognises the language used by the customer; test language changes in the playground.

For each language, make sure the instructions are translated correctly and keep the same tone and content as the original.

> [!TIP]
> **Language detection & per-direction voices**
> The assistant recognizes the language the caller speaks among the ones you configured, and switches to it when the caller speaks it clearly in full sentences or asks for it. To avoid switching on a misheard word, mid-call it first asks the caller to repeat. Inbound and outbound assistants can also use different voices.
