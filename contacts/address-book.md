# Customer directory

Your centralised archive of customers and guests

_The customer directory is the centralised archive of everyone who has interacted with your business through Yourang. Every contact has a profile with a History of all their interactions on every channel: calls, WhatsApp, website chat, Facebook and Instagram, bookings and orders. You don't have to enter anything by hand._

## What is saved automatically <a id="cosa-viene-salvato"></a>

At the end of every call handled by the voice assistant, Yourang updates the customer profile with the relevant information that emerged from the conversation. No manual work is required: the system recognises the caller's number and matches the data to the corresponding profile.

- **First and last name** — Detected from the customer's introduction during the call or entered manually.
- **Phone number** — Automatically recorded from the caller's number.
- **Date and time of last interaction** — Updated with every new call, so you always know when you last heard from the customer.
- **Call summary** — A short AI-generated summary with the main points of the conversation.

> [!TIP]
> **Returning customers**
> When a returning customer calls, the assistant can recognise them and personalise the greeting or recall previous information, making the experience more welcoming.

## The customer profile <a id="profilo-cliente"></a>

Every contact in the directory has a profile page. At the top you find the main details and, right below, the History; further down are the extra information, the table of calls and the AI summaries.

**Contact details**
: First and last name, main and secondary phone, email and address. Edit them at any time with «Edit contact».

**Lists**
: The lists the contact belongs to, with the automatic WhatsApp lists set apart from the others.

**Source**
: How the contact came into the directory: manual, CSV import, AI call, lead form, WhatsApp address book, WhatsApp chat, Facebook/Instagram chat or website chat.

**History**
: Every interaction with the contact, on every channel, newest first.

**More information**
: The custom fields filled in for this contact.

**All calls**
: The full table of calls, where you can select up to 10 calls and generate an AI summary.

**AI summaries**
: The summaries you have generated for this contact.

## The History <a id="storico"></a>

The History puts every interaction with the contact in a single sequence: calls, WhatsApp conversations, website chats, Facebook and Instagram, bookings and orders. Each row shows the channel, date and time, whether it was inbound or outbound, who handled it (assistant or operator), the outcome, a two-line summary you can expand, and the duration or number of messages.

- **Filters** — Switch from «All» to a single channel: Calls, WhatsApp, Website, Facebook/Instagram, Bookings, Orders.
- **Period** — With «Period» you look at one stretch of time only: the last 7 or 30 days, the last 3 months, the last year, or the days you pick on the calendar. The History scrolls inside its box and loads older interactions by itself when you reach the end.
- **Summaries** — For calls it is the AI summary. For chats it is generated after a quiet spell and updated if the conversation picks up again. Bookings and orders show the date, party size or total.
- **Translation** — With «Translate» you read the summary in your language; you can always go back to the original.
- **Unanswered attempts** — Several consecutive unanswered call attempts appear as a single row, which you can open with «Show the attempts».
- **Details** — «Open» takes you to the call, conversation, booking or order; from there «Back to contact», at the top, brings you back here in one click.

## Contacts created automatically <a id="contatti-automatici"></a>

Anyone who writes to you on WhatsApp becomes a contact on their own, with the name of their WhatsApp profile. It also happens when a new person receives a template from a campaign or a workflow. A name you entered yourself is never overwritten.

- **Automatic WhatsApp list** — New contacts go into a WhatsApp list, marked with a badge in Contacts › Lists. The owner and admins choose there whether to have one list or one per connected number.
- **Facebook and Instagram** — In the inbox, from the «…» menu of a conversation, «Link to contact» ties it to an existing contact. The link is also made on its own when the customer writes their phone number in the chat; if the number belongs to no contact, the contact is created.
- **Source** — These contacts have «WhatsApp chat» or «Facebook/Instagram chat» as their source.

> [!NOTE]
> **What you see depends on your permissions**
> Each channel in the History requires its own area: Calls for calls, Messages for WhatsApp, website chat and Facebook/Instagram, Bookings for bookings, Shop for orders. Anyone without access to an area sees neither those interactions nor the matching filter.

## How to view a profile <a id="come-consultare"></a>

1. **Open the Contacts section** — From the sidebar select "Contacts" to access the full directory.
2. **Search for the customer** — Use the search bar to find a customer by first name, last name, or phone number.
3. **Open the profile** — Click the customer's name to view the full profile with all information and history.

> [!NOTE]
> **Privacy and data handling**
> All customer data is processed in compliance with the GDPR. Make sure you have informed your customers about the processing of personal data in line with current regulations.
