# Subscription plans

Comparison of available plans: what each includes and who it's for.

_Yourang offers multiple plans to suit properties of different sizes. All plans include the complete dashboard, the basic voice assistant features, and access to support; what changes is the included call volume and service level._

## How to choose the right plan <a id="criteri-di-scelta"></a>

- **Estimate call volume** — How many calls do you receive on average per month? How long do they last? A property with 100 three-minute calls needs a different plan than one with 1,000 five-minute calls.
- **Consider seasonal peaks** — If your business has marked seasonality (summer for coastal hotels, weekends for restaurants), consider a plan with sufficient margin during peak months or use the wallet to cover peaks.
- **Evaluate advanced features** — Integrations with management systems, custom criteria, multi-number, multiple departments: these features are available starting from intermediate plans.
- **Think about support** — Higher plans include dedicated onboarding, priority support channels, and faster response times. An important choice if you run a business with continuous operational needs.

> [!NOTE]
> **Always up-to-date pricing**
> The complete price list, with monthly and annual prices, is visible in the Plan management section of the dashboard. Prices and inclusions may vary over time: in case of changes, active customers are notified in advance.

## Annual or monthly? <a id="annuale-vs-mensile"></a>

The annual subscription is more cost-effective than the monthly one for the same plan and gives you certainty of a fixed cost for twelve months. The monthly subscription is more flexible, ideal if you are evaluating the platform or if your business has short-term characteristics. You can move from monthly to yearly at any time from the Change plan page: the yearly plan starts when the month you have already paid ends, and is charged then. A yearly plan cannot go back to monthly.

## Promotional codes and discounts <a id="promozioni-e-codici"></a>

If you have received a promotional code (for example from a partner, a trade show, or our sales team), enter it in the Promo code field when you choose your plan. There are two kinds of code, and the field recognises which one you typed; it also accepts a friend's referral code (see below).

An activation code takes an amount off the one-time Training & setup fee. It is used the next time you pay Training & setup; you can also type it on the payment page itself when you pay Training & setup. Each organization can use up to 3 activation codes in total, one for each activation: once a code has been used, the field stays available for the next one, but you cannot add a new code while another is still waiting to be used.

A plan code takes a percentage or a fixed amount off a plan: a voice plan, or a WhatsApp or Meta pack. It can be limited to certain plans and to monthly, quarterly or yearly billing, and it lasts for the first invoice, a set number of months, or for as long as the subscription runs. It is used by your next purchase of a plan it covers; the discount already shows in the order summary, before you pay. You can hold one plan code at a time, alongside an activation code. If you change the billing frequency of an add-on whose discount is still running, the discount ends with the change, unless the code lasts for as long as the subscription runs and also covers the new frequency.

Until a payment uses it, you can change or remove a code from the same field. While a payment with the code is still being processed (for example a SEPA direct debit), it can no longer be changed.
