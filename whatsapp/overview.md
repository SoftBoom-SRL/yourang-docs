# WhatsApp Business in Yourang

What you can do with WhatsApp and why it's a natural complement to the voice assistant.

_WhatsApp is today the channel customers prefer for quick requests, confirmations, and informal bookings. Yourang gives you the tools to manage it professionally, integrated with the rest of the platform._

## Why WhatsApp <a id="vantaggi"></a>

- **Convenience for the customer** — Many people prefer to write a message rather than call. WhatsApp lets you capture these requests without forcing the customer to change channel.
- **Asynchrony** — Unlike a call, a WhatsApp conversation can move at the customer's pace: your team responds when they can.
- **Traceability** — Everything stays in writing: no ambiguity about what was said, when, or by whom. Particularly useful for complex requests.
- **Continuity with the call** — A conversation started on the phone can continue on WhatsApp (to send a link, a confirmation, a map) and vice versa.

## The WhatsApp sections of the panel <a id="componenti"></a>

- **Chat** — The interface where your team manages conversations in real time.
- **Automations** — Automatic replies for typical messages, after-hours, or to route the conversation.
- **Templates** — Message templates approved by WhatsApp, required to write to customers who haven't written first. You can prepare them before connecting a number: they are saved as drafts and sent to Meta for review once the number is connected.
- **Contacts and lists** — Dedicated WhatsApp address book, segmentation for targeted campaigns.
- **AI agents** — AI agents that reply to WhatsApp conversations automatically and hand over to the team when needed.

> [!NOTE]
> **You need a number and Meta approval**
> To activate WhatsApp Business in Yourang you must have a dedicated number (not already used on consumer WhatsApp) and complete the Meta approval process. The support team guides the initial setup.

## Billing at Meta and the payment method <a id="fatturazione-meta"></a>

WhatsApp has two separate bills. Meta charges your business directly for the messages your business starts (marketing, utility and authentication templates), on a payment method attached to your WhatsApp account at Meta; the platform bills its own fee with your subscription. The “Billing at Meta” card in the WhatsApp Business settings shows what Meta sees, with one of three states:

- **Payment method working** — Meta is accepting your template messages, so a payment method is set up.
- **Payment method not verified yet** — No billable message has been sent yet, so Meta has not confirmed a payment method. Before your first campaign, make sure one is attached to your WhatsApp account.
- **Payment method needed at Meta** — Meta refused a message with error 131042 (no usable payment method, or the card was declined). Templates and campaigns keep failing until it is fixed at Meta.

Meta often accepts a template and refuses it a moment later, so the notice can appear after a send that looked fine. The card then shows the date of the last refusal and the reason given by Meta. Once you have fixed the payment method at Meta, click “I've fixed it”: the card goes back to “not verified yet” and updates when Meta delivers the next template; if Meta refuses again, the notice turns red again. A new card can take 10–15 minutes to activate at Meta, so wait before sending a test template.
