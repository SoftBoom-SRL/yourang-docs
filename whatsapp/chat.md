# Real-time chat

How the team manages WhatsApp conversations from the panel.

_The Chat section is the unified inbox for all WhatsApp conversations. It works like a messaging client, with a few extra tools designed for team collaboration._

## The interface <a id="interfaccia"></a>

On the left, the list of conversations, with the most recent activity first, each with an avatar with the customer's initials, unread badges and the last message; in the middle, the selected chat. When there is something to add to the name and number in the chat header (recent calls, the next reservation, or a number that is not in your contacts yet), a “Customer card” button opens it: it shows the contact, the recent calls and the next reservation, and offers “Save as contact”. A template you have sent appears in the chat with its header, text, footer and buttons, just as the customer sees it.

## Assignment and collaboration <a id="assegnazione"></a>

- **Auto-assignment** — New conversations are distributed among online operators according to configurable rules (round-robin, to a specific department, based on keywords).
- **Manual transfer** — An operator can hand a conversation to a more suitable colleague, preserving the history and notes.
- **Internal notes** — Visible only to the team, not to the customer. Useful for notes, context, and flagging.
- **Quick replies** — Saved phrases recalled with a shortcut: 'hours', 'address', 'directions'. They speed up repetitive replies.

## Conversation status <a id="stato-conversazione"></a>

Each conversation has a status: open, waiting for customer, waiting for team, closed. The status updates automatically but can be forced to organise the workflow. Closed conversations can be reopened if the customer writes again.

## Unread conversations <a id="non-lette"></a>

Under the search box, the conversation list has two tabs: “All” and “Unread”. “Unread” shows only the open conversations with messages nobody has opened yet; when there are none it reads “No unread conversations”.

To bring a conversation back to the team's attention, open it and press the envelope icon “Mark as unread” in the header: the conversation shows as new again for the whole team and the chat closes on your screen. The icon is not offered on conversations marked as Done. The number next to the WhatsApp conversations in the side menu counts the open conversations with unread messages.

When a customer is waiting for a reply you also get an alert, and it names the customer and the channel (WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook). If nobody answers, the alert comes back with updated wording after 15 minutes, after 1 hour and after 4 hours (“for over 15 minutes”, “for over an hour”, “for over 4 hours”).

> [!TIP]
> **The 24-hour window**
> WhatsApp only allows you to write freely to a customer in the 24 hours after one of their messages. Beyond that, you must use an approved template. The panel clearly indicates this when you're about to send.

> [!TIP]
> **Formatting messages**
> Use the B, I, S and code buttons under the message box (or Ctrl/⌘+B and Ctrl/⌘+I) for bold, italic, strikethrough and monospace text. The same formatting works in template bodies.

## Contact tags <a id="etichette-contatto"></a>

Tags are labels you put on a customer, such as “VIP” or “To call back”. A tag is saved on the contact with that phone number (the contact is created if it is not in your list yet), so it shows on every conversation with that number.

- **Add a tag** — In the chat header, under the customer's name, press “Tags”, then pick an existing tag or type a name and choose “Create “name””. A name can be up to 50 characters.
- **Remove a tag** — Press the × on the tag in the chat header.
- **See the tags** — The tags appear as coloured pills in the chat header; each row of the list shows up to three, the rest as “+2”, “+3”…
- **Filter the list** — Next to the “All” and “Unread” tabs, press “Tags” and tick the tags you want: the list shows the conversations that carry any of them. You can filter by up to 20 tags at a time; the × next to the button clears the filter.

Members with read-only access to messages see the tags but cannot change them.

In Workflows, the “Reply on WhatsApp” and “Send WhatsApp message” steps have two optional fields, “Add tags to the recipient” and “Remove tags from the recipient”: pick existing tags or type new names. The tags change only after the message has been sent; if tagging fails, the message stays sent and the step does not fail.
