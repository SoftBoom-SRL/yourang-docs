# Location and address

Your physical business address and service coverage area.

_The address of your business is used by the AI assistant to give precise directions to customers calling to visit you or to request deliveries in your area._

## Search with Google Places <a id="ricerca-google-places"></a>

Start typing the address in the main field: the Google Places autocomplete suggests real addresses and auto-fills city, province, ZIP code and country. You can always edit individual fields manually if needed.

## Required fields <a id="campi-obbligatori"></a>

- Address (street, number)
- City
- Country

> [!TIP]
> **Location status**
> At the bottom of the card you'll see a green confirmation icon when the address is complete. If required fields are missing, a yellow warning appears: complete them to give the AI accurate answers.

## Other locations <a id="altri-luoghi"></a>

If you have more than one office, branch or shop, add the others in the "Other locations" section, below the address. The main site stays the address at the top; here you list the others.

- Name (required), for example "Milan office".
- Address, with Google Places autocomplete.
- The location's phone number.
- Opening hours as free text, for example "Mon-Fri 9-13 and 15-19, Saturday 9-12".
- You can add up to 20 locations; press "Save locations" to confirm.

> [!NOTE]
> **How the assistant uses them**
> The AI assistant uses these locations in calls and messages when a customer asks where you are or when a location is open. If you change them by asking the dashboard assistant, keep in mind that it saves the whole list: tell it every location you want to keep. Other locations are information only: they do not get their own agents, phone numbers or calendars.
