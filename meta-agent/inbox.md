# Inbox and takeover

Monitor Instagram and Facebook messages and comments and take over a conversation when a person is needed.

_The inbox is the single mailbox where the direct messages and comments from Instagram and Facebook handled by the agent come together. From here you follow the conversations and, whenever you want, answer instead of the agent._

## The inbox view <a id="vista-inbox"></a>

Open “Inbox” from the left-hand menu. On the left there is the list of conversations with a bar to search them by name; in the centre the selected conversation with its full message history. Each conversation shows the platform (Instagram or Facebook) and whether it is currently handled by the AI or by a person.

## Two channels: Messages and Comments <a id="messaggi-e-commenti"></a>

There are two tabs at the top of the list. “Messages” shows private conversations (DMs). “Comments” shows the comments under your posts: each entry carries the originating post, the author and an “Open post” link that takes you straight to the content on Instagram or Facebook. In the Comments tab the reply box writes a public comment, not a private message: the placeholder reminds you.

## Filters and search <a id="filtri"></a>

- **Platform filter** — The “All”, “Facebook” and “Instagram” pills narrow the list down to a single channel.
- **Search by name** — The search bar filters conversations by the person's name.
- **Interaction counter** — Next to the tabs there's the pill with the remaining interactions: this is where they get spent, so this is where you watch it change.

## Taking over and handing back to the AI <a id="presa-in-carico"></a>

- **Take over** — Press “Take over” to pause the automation and answer manually. Until you take over, the reply box stays read-only (see the “Take over to reply” button).
- **Write and send** — After taking over, type in the reply field and press “Send”. The customer receives your message as if it came from the profile.
- **Hand back to the AI** — When you're done, press “Hand back to AI”: the agent resumes replying automatically in that conversation.

> [!NOTE]
> **Your replies consume interactions too**
> Every message sent from the inbox — written by the agent or by you after taking over — counts as one plan interaction.

## Deleting a conversation <a id="elimina-conversazione"></a>

The bin icon removes the thread and its messages from your inbox. Nothing is deleted on Facebook or Instagram: the conversation still exists there, and a new message from the same person recreates the thread from scratch. Use it to tidy up, not to really delete something.

## The 24-hour window <a id="finestra-24-ore"></a>

> [!WARNING]
> **The 24-hour window**
> Meta only allows you to reply freely to a customer within 24 hours of their last message. Beyond that window free replies are no longer allowed without special rules: if you need to handle a chat manually, do it within 24 hours.

## Check that everything works (final test) <a id="verifica"></a>

Before considering the agent “live”, run this full test using a second account (for example a colleague's or your own personal profile).

1. **Send a test DM on Instagram** — From another account, send a message to your professional Instagram profile. The agent should reply within the delay you configured and the conversation should appear in the inbox.
2. **Send a test DM on Facebook** — Repeat by messaging your Facebook Page from Messenger.
3. **Try a comment** — Comment on one of your public posts with a question: the agent should reply publicly. If you try a rude comment, it should hide it (if you enabled moderation).
4. **Try another language** — Send a message in English: the reply must come back in English.
5. **Try taking over** — Open a conversation in the inbox, press “Take over”, send a manual reply and then “Hand back to AI”.
6. **If something doesn't work** — Go to the “Troubleshooting” page: it covers the most frequent cases in order of likelihood.
