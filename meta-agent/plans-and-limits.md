# Plans, interactions and limits

Which plans include the Meta agent, how many interactions you get and what consumes them.

_The Meta agent is a feature of the higher paid plans and works with a number of “interactions” included per billing period. Understanding how they are counted saves you the surprise of an agent that suddenly stops replying._

## Which plans include it <a id="piani"></a>

- **Plus** — Includes the Meta agent with 100 interactions per billing period.
- **Enterprise** — Includes the Meta agent with 500 interactions per billing period.
- **Free, Pro and trial periods** — Do not include the Meta agent. In the dashboard the counter shows a padlock and, when clicked, an invitation to upgrade.

> [!NOTE]
> **Custom plans**
> Some commercial agreements include unlimited interactions: in that case you'll see the infinity symbol instead of a number.

## Where you see how many are left <a id="interazioni"></a>

The counter appears in two places: at the top of the Meta agent page (with a label and progress bar) and, in compact form, in the inbox header. Click it to open the summary: interactions used out of those included, and the date the count resets. The colour turns from neutral to amber once you have used 70%, and to red when you hit zero.

## What consumes an interaction <a id="cosa-consuma"></a>

- A direct message sent by the agent.
- A public reply posted under a comment.
- A comment hidden by automatic moderation.
- A message you send from the inbox after taking over the conversation.

> [!TIP]
> **The reply delay saves you interactions**
> With a DM delay, three messages fired off by the customer get a single reply: one interaction instead of three. It is the simplest way to make your included interactions last longer.

## What happens when they run out <a id="quota-esaurita"></a>

The agent stops replying: messages and comments keep arriving in the inbox, but no automatic reply goes out until the period renews or you move to a higher plan. The counter turns red and the summary explains it. You can still reply by hand from the inbox, bearing in mind those replies also need available interactions.

## Limits imposed by Meta <a id="limiti-meta"></a>

- **24-hour window** — You can only reply freely to a person within 24 hours of their last message.
- **Authorisation to renew** — Facebook occasionally asks you to repeat the authorisation: when the agent stops replying for no reason, reconnect the integration.
- **Request rate limits** — Meta limits how many requests can be made in a given time. The platform spreads them out on its own: during very heavy traffic a reply may arrive a few seconds later.

## Beta status <a id="beta"></a>

Next to the agent type you'll see the “Beta” label. The feature is usable in production but still evolving: some capabilities (for example handling voice messages and images in the inbox) are marked as “Coming soon” and will be added in future updates.
